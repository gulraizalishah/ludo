Keep it fun

  • Only stake money you can afford to lose. Never play with money you need for rent, food or bills.
  • Decide how much you will spend before you start, and stop when you reach it.
  • Do not chase losses. Winning back money you lost is a bad reason to keep playing.
  • Take breaks. Do not play when you are upset, tired or drunk.

Tools in the game

  • Daily stake limit. Open "My limits" in the lobby. Lowering the limit works at once. Raising or removing it takes 24 hours, so you cannot change your mind in the heat of the moment.
  • Take a break. Pause your account for 1, 7 or 30 days. You cannot start matches while paused, but you can still withdraw your money.

Signs it is becoming a problem

Spending more time or money than you planned, hiding it from people close to you, borrowing to play, or feeling anxious when you are not playing. If this sounds familiar, take a long break and talk to someone you trust. Many countries have free, confidential helplines for gambling problems: search for one in your country.
