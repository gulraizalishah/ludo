1. Who can play

You must be 18 years old or older. One person may have one account. You are responsible for making sure that playing for money is allowed where you live.

2. Your account

Keep your password private. You are responsible for everything done with your account. Tell us at once if you think someone else has used it.

3. Money

  • You deposit [TRX / USDT] to the address shown in the game. Only send the currency shown, on the TRON network. Coins sent in any other way cannot be recovered.
  • When you join a table, your stake is held until the match ends. The winner receives the pool minus the platform fee. The fee percentage is shown before you join a table.
  • Withdrawals are paid to the TRON address you give us. Check it carefully: payments to a wrong address cannot be reversed. We may apply limits, waiting times, or manual checks to protect players and the service.
  • We may refuse or reverse a payout, and freeze a balance, if we find a breach of these terms.

4. Fair play

It is not allowed to use more than one account, to play against yourself or to pass money between accounts, to use bots or scripts, or to exploit bugs. We may cancel matches, freeze balances and close accounts that break these rules.

5. Leaving a match

If you stop playing, the server plays your turns for a short time and then removes you from the match. You lose your stake.

6. Risk

You can lose the money you stake. Results depend on the dice and on your choices. Only play with money you can afford to lose. See Responsible play for tools to set limits or take a break.

7. The service

We try to keep the game running but cannot promise it will always be available. Blockchain payments can be delayed by the network.
