Practice mode. No money involved yet.

House rules
  • Leaving base: roll a 6. Sixes and captures give an extra turn; three sixes in a row lose the turn.
  • Blockade: 2+ of your pieces on one square (not a star) block everyone else. One blockade per player.
  • Breaking a blockade: a group of the same size right behind it, rolling the exact number. This works from a star square too. You can open your own any time, but must if no other piece can move.
  • Home lane: locked 🔒 until you capture an opponent piece. Until then your pieces keep circling.
